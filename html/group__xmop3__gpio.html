◆ mop3_cio_digitalWrite()

Sexpr_t mop3_cio_digitalWrite ( Lamb & lamb,
Sexpr_t sexpr,
Sexpr_t env_exec )

Write digital pin HIGH/LOW. Level is Scheme-truthy (only #f is LOW) with one pragmatic extension: a NUMERIC ZERO also drives LOW. So (digitalWrite p #f) and (digitalWrite p 0) both give LOW; (digitalWrite p #t) and (digitalWrite p 1) give HIGH.
